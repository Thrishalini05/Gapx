What is Research Gap Discovery?

Research Gap Discovery is designed to help researchers analyze existing scholarly literature and identify potential research gaps using semantic analysis and AI-assisted reasoning.

How It Works

1. Provide Research

Upload a research PDF or enter your research idea.

2. Search Literature

Select scholarly sources and retrieve relevant literature.

3. Analyze

Compare relevant papers using semantic analysis.

4. Discover Gaps

Identify candidate research gaps and possible improvements.
